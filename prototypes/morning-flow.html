Pattern comparison

How firm should the boundary feel?

Market patterns suggest a range from gentle friction to task-first gating. The middle path is a starting recommendation; the owner’s preference remains open.

A · Gentle

Delay, then choose

Each app open gets a short pause and “Do you want to continue?” prompt. No routine needed to proceed.

✓ Easy recovery, low setup · △ Less reliable against autopilot
B · Best fit to explore

Routine unlock + bounded pass

Selected apps wait for required steps. A temporary release could relock afterward; its duration and allowance remain open product choices.

✓ Morning is meaningfully protected · △ Self-report and recovery rules need clear trust boundaries
C · Strict

Hard schedule + no same-day edit

Routine gate stays in place through a latest-unlock time; changes take effect tomorrow. Emergency route stays visible.

✓ Stronger friction · △ More setup, higher risk when the block misfires
Market inspiration

Borrow the useful shape.

These references change the morning in distinct ways. EKKO can adapt the interaction without inheriting every tradeoff.

Do FirstTask-first gate

Turn a morning list into the access condition. Adapt the clear “what’s left?” progress; preserve honesty that a tap is self-report.

JomoComplete Habits + Squads

Make user-defined steps unlock apps and friends encourage. Share completion and streak by choice; keep app activity and routine details private.

LockitChecklist + temporary release

Use a custom list and bounded pass with relock. Keep the recovery rule legible; avoid making exceptions feel punitive.

ScreenZenDelay + strict modes

Offer a lighter “pause and decide” mode and a firmer mode later. More friction adds configuration and recovery complexity.

Captain review · product direction

What should EKKO feel like?

Captain’s product direction

Confirmed directions are shown as current choices. Use the open controls to react to unresolved details; nothing queues until you submit this card.

one consolidated card

1. What counts as completing the routine?

Recommendation: retain the current direction; it gives people control while keeping a clear completion rule.

2. Which apps should wait?

Recommendation: keep selected apps as the default; defer all-apps-on-wake until feasibility is known.

3. When does the morning pause start?

Recommendation: retain the daily 4:00 a.m. start already chosen.

4. What do friends see, and what tone should encouragement use?

Current direction: completed friends and their current streak. Recommendation: keep sharing discreet and supportive; the exact tone is open for review.

5. How should exceptions and missed mornings work?

Known: a deliberate full-day bypass ends the streak. Recommendation: explore a clear temporary override, but decide its rules and unplanned missed-day behavior separately.

6. Should EKKO ask for optional proof?

Recommendation: keep proof out of the first concept; it adds privacy and setup costs without verifying completion.
